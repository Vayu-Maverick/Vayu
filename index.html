01 THE CHALLENGE

Some places are still
hard to reach.

Moving very heavy cargo can depend on runways, roads, ports, or costly specialist aircraft. Vayu explores another way to think about access.

↗

01 / AIR

Infrastructure-bound

Conventional cargo aircraft rely on suitable airports and ground-handling infrastructure.

⌁

02 / LIFT

Specialist capacity

Heavy-lift helicopters can reach remote sites, but their payload and operating economics are challenging.

◎

03 / ACCESS

Last-mile gaps

Road and sea links may not serve isolated, landlocked, or disaster-affected locations when access matters most.

02 THE VAYU CONCEPT

A different route
to heavy lift.

The proposal brings buoyant lift, distributed electric propulsion, and a runway-independent cargo concept together in one early-stage design study.

01

Lift & structure

A proposed 240-metre, non-pressurised teardrop hull with an internal lightweight frame. The concept pairs hydrogen primary lift with a helium safety envelope.

240 M HULL TARGET
02

Propulsion & energy

Three electric variable-pitch propulsors are arranged around the hull, with a dorsal solar array proposed to assist onboard energy needs.

ELECTRIC · SOLAR-ASSISTED
03

Cargo delivery

Winch-lowered, ISO-compatible cargo pods are intended to support loading and delivery without a conventional runway.

UP TO 4 WINCHES PROPOSED
!

All systems shown are conceptual. Detailed design, engineering analysis, testing, and regulatory work remain ahead.

04 POSSIBLE APPLICATIONS

Designed around
hard-to-reach work.

Potential applications described in the proposal. Real-world use would depend on feasibility, safety, and regulatory review.

01 / INFRASTRUCTURE

Remote construction

Explore delivery of large equipment to mountain, offshore, and off-grid project sites.

02 / HUMANITARIAN

Disaster response

Consider a new heavy-lift option for reaching communities cut off from ground transport.

03 / ENERGY & RESOURCES

Remote operations

Study how oversized machinery and supplies might reach sites without road or port access.

04 / FREIGHT

Alternative logistics

Investigate buoyant lift as a complement to conventional transport networks.
